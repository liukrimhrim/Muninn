Rules used in this prototype
  1. Use the larger exercise to expose several pieces of knowledge. A final outcome alone gives no automatic credit to its ingredients.
  2. Repair an observed component gap first. Keep other gaps visible; do not blame every prerequisite.
  3. If the cause is uncertain, ask a focused question about an unresolved component. Parts come before their connection, then selecting the rule. This is a fixed, inspectable ordering, not a claim of optimal information gain.
  4. After feedback, check the repaired target without help on a different prompt. Then return to a different larger exercise.
  5. If all component checks pass but integration fails, practice connecting the parts. A slip remains possible.
  6. New failures can contradict earlier successes. Preserve both and change the next practice choice.
  7. Stop when the diagnosis or time budget is exhausted. Keep unresolved alternatives visible.
  8. After a fully demonstrated independent larger answer, defer separate prompts for this session and reserve a later integrated check. This is a rule to inspect, not validated review compression.
Illustrative policy: 3 diagnostic questions by default; a later check after 7 simulated days. The authored variants share a solution pattern; they do not establish broad transfer. These are design choices, not measured optimal values. No FSRS updates, saved progress, external requests, or background jobs. Reloading clears the session.
