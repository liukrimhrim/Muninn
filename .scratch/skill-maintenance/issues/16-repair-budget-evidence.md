# What supports bounded repair and useful practice when time and evidence are limited?

## Question

Review primary evidence about repeated errors, feedback and worked examples, diagnostic versus teaching prompts, bounded learner effort and delayed checks. Separate evidence-backed constraints from operational starting policies for interruption, repeated unsuccessful repair, overdue work and new material. Do not invent an optimal prompt cap, backlog quota, efficiency coefficient or universal mastery threshold.

## Answer

The [focused research](../research/bounded-repair-and-practice-budget.md) checks six primary studies, with its abstract-only access boundary stated for one. Retrieval, feedback, worked examples and delayed checks are supported within their studied tasks; universal diagnostic/repair caps and backlog quotas are not. Diagnosis itself can teach. The note distinguishes misread, content/assessment fault, supported gap, uncertain cause and assisted response, with eight proposed acceptance cases.

Read-only review of the design identified five material corrections: assisted checks need a non-looping exit; newly revealed concerns must not extend priority indefinitely; undated ready work needs an honest selection-age anchor; browser presentation requires reservation/acknowledgement uncertainty; and verification needs an explicit binding to earlier completed teaching. These are incorporated into the [proposal](../everyday-learning-design.md) and cases. Research resolved; the operating limits remain proposed, without a claim of optimality or learning benefit.
