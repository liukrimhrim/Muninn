## Question

What do primary empirical studies show about retrieval, spacing, transfer, procedural practice and assessment format? Which findings bear on compressing review, and which do not establish cross-concept or cross-format credit?

## Answer

Primary studies support retrieval, spacing and some transfer under studied conditions, but do not establish automatic prerequisite or cross-format credit for this domain. The report specifies delayed unseen assessments, total cost accounting and a narrow review-omission experiment; KARL's published efficiency denominator is posttest latency.
