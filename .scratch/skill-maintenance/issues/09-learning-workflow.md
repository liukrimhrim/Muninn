## Question

What is the first complete workflow from learning material to retained knowledge? Specify supported inputs, how knowledge components and relationships are created and checked, how exercises are authored or derived, how the learner edits them, and how practice and feedback lead to later reviews. Determine what the learner controls and what the system may automate. The same flow must support facts, relationships and connected structures across subjects; procedural execution is needed only for targets that require it.
