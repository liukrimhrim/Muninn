# What supports fresh, comparable checks without confusing recall and transfer?

## Question

Review primary evidence for exercise-family variation, retrieval versus recognition, answer exposure, comparable outcome interpretation and aging evidence. Identify what a general learning app can specify now without pretending to have calibrated item difficulty or a fitted mastery model. Preserve simple direct recall as a legitimate target. Explain where the evidence cannot justify a fixed threshold or automatic classification.

## Answer

The [focused research](../research/fresh-checks-and-evidence-age.md) inspects five primary sources and separates repeated exact-cue recall, surface variation, new instances and changed demands. Source matching cannot establish equal difficulty; old observations remain dated evidence; answer exposure, response independence and delayed eligibility require separate records. The literature does not determine an exposure cooldown or a mastery ranking. Eight discriminating examples and concrete record implications feed the [everyday-learning proposal](../everyday-learning-design.md).

Read-only review of the resulting draft identified four corrections: do not reject reuse of a taught method, persist exposure eligibility across session boundaries, hide answer-bearing paraphrases as well as quotes, and preview actual correction effects including a justified historical E→S. These were incorporated with acceptance cases. Research resolved; product policy approval and semantic/runtime qualification are not claimed. Context: `codex/research/observability-campaign`.
