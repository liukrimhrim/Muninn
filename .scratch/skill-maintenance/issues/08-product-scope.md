## Question

Is this a general memorization-oriented learning system or a product focused on one operational subject?

## Answer

The user explicitly settled this question:

> to be clear: i want to build a general system that focuses on memorization oriented learning, not just for k8s

Design a general system for memorization-oriented learning across subjects. Preserve the original needs for connected knowledge, meaningful granularity, targeted practice and fewer redundant reviews. Kubernetes illustrates procedural knowledge only. Hands-on execution is appropriate when the learner's target requires it; it is not a universal requirement.

The earlier operational focus was the coordinator's assumption. It does not constrain the product. Choosing small validation examples and a limited first release remains possible, but those choices must preserve the general learning model.

## Comments

The coordinator corrected the proposed design, glossary, map and remaining decision questions. Existing research and the Kubernetes spike remain useful evidence with narrower scope; they do not constitute a complete design or validation of the general workflow.
