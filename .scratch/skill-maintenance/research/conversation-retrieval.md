## What the previous synthesis still compressed

The earlier audit restored broad categories, but a category name did not preserve every decision inside it. These distinctions deserve explicit treatment:

1. **Knowing when to use knowledge.** Recalling a rule, carrying it out, and recognizing the situation that calls for it are different demands. The original examples include recognizing an algorithmic pattern and choosing an operational action from symptoms.
2. **Topology means more than grouping.** The discussion includes relationships that jointly constrain each other, common structures across clusters, reconstructing reasoning, and deriving a new consequence. Listing members of a group does not express all of those.
3. **Some details should be fluent; others can be reconstructed.** The source argues both for deriving details from a smaller set of principles and for remembering frequently needed facts directly. This requires a goal-dependent choice, not a single mastery label.
4. **Successful exercises can reveal repair needs.** A correct final outcome can coexist with hesitation, irrelevant actions, or help. The source explicitly proposes using those observations to choose a short repair exercise.
5. **Repair may concern selection or interpretation.** A learner can remember an action and execute it but fail to select it, interpret its result, or verify a conclusion. “Missing fact versus missing connection” is not a complete diagnosis vocabulary.
6. **Compression is conditional and personal.** The source discusses task-specific actual practice, fractional effects, differences between learners who benefit from implicit practice and those who need explicit practice, and learning those effects from outcomes.
7. **Coverage changes after every answer.** The set-cover illustration is only a starting point. Coverage is uncertain, strength varies, history matters, time passes, and an answer changes what should be asked next.
8. **Structural diagnosis is an alternative to independent mastery estimates.** Knowledge Space Theory is proposed as inference over plausible combinations of knowledge. Its treatment of noisy or contradictory performance remains an explicit research question.
9. **The graph can evolve with the learner and material.** The hybrid proposal includes progressively constructing, refining, and testing the representation, not just generating cards once. It also includes attaching new knowledge to what the learner already knows.
10. **Exercise delivery has its own job.** Generation, execution, assessment, and corrective feedback belong in the design. A prompt selector alone does not supply a learning experience. Domain-specific delivery does not make the core product domain-specific.
11. **The research backlog is broader than FSRS integration.** Math Academy's diagnosis and compression, ALEKS/KST, the Knowledge Spaces toolkit, semantic prediction, and contextual authoring tools serve different research questions. Reviewing one scheduler cannot stand in for all of them.

These are coverage findings, not a ranked list of proven mechanisms. Some were absent; others existed as a word, generic field, or caution without preserving the source's concrete distinction.

## Consequences for the current plan

The report restores distinctions; it does not close the design. The linked decisions now need to cover these concrete questions:

- **Workflow:** How does new material connect to prior knowledge, how are candidate relations checked and refined, and what generation, delivery, grading, and feedback are needed for a whole learning loop?
- **Representation:** Can targets distinguish recalling, reconstructing, selecting from cues, checking applicability, combining knowledge, and interpreting results without multiplying unnecessary state? How do contrasts, generalization, and joint constraints appear in exercises?
- **Inference:** Can partial evidence from both success and failure localize useful reinforcement? What changes when actual solution paths differ, several gaps coexist, or structural prerequisites are only hypotheses? What can be personalized about implicit practice?
- **Selection:** How should time, uncertain overlap, prior practice, current answers, changing format, and fluent-versus-reconstructed targets affect the sequence? How do diagnostic questions stop before they consume the learning session?
- **Validation:** Include held-out assessment of selection and integration, successful tasks with local problems, wrong answers with distinct causes, and two exercises that mention the same topics but actually elicit different knowledge. Report useful diagnosis, later retention, and total effort separately.

The existing evidence-policy demo cannot answer most of these questions. A later diagnosis/coverage spike should preserve the recovered fixtures and compare plausible policies; it should not claim superiority by comparing only against indiscriminate graph credit.
