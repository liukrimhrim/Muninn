# One learning history, consistent next actions

Status: first-release lifecycle contract finalized in [Design Baseline 1](design-baseline.md), 2026-10-16. This contains the cross-contract audit patches, later correction/exposure refinements and module-review ownership corrections. The companion tracking contract owns planned comparison lifecycle and qualified aggregation. No implementation or measured learning benefit is claimed.

This document owns admission, authoring intent, ordinary attempt ownership/order and exposure recovery. The [compatibility contract](compatibility-design.md) still owns identity, immutable history, transactions and restore; [everyday learning](everyday-learning-design.md) owns selection, bounded diagnosis and repair. Named payload additions below complete the unreleased format 1 proposal. No additional service, generic workflow engine or released-format migration is needed.

## 1. Selecting goals does not create a review backlog

There are two different kinds of state: each enrollment says what the learner wants to practise there; one library-wide history/schedule describes each exact target. They must not be collapsed into a single curriculum percentage.

| Learner action or state | Saved effect | Meaning in the interface |
|---|---|---|
| Import material | Save source and draft/accepted outline; no learning observations or dated reviews | Available material |
| Keep targets in the outline | Add exact targets to `enrollment.goal_order`; new targets start `unstarted` | Selected goals; visible in the goal denominator |
| Choose new work in the session preview | Admit only the chosen targets, mark their enrollment entries `active`, create an initial need if no schedule exists | Started practice; no due timestamp until evidence or completed study establishes one |
| Begin an unfamiliar target through study | Admit it before delivery; save actual study separately | Study started/completed, not demonstrated knowledge |
| Demonstrate a target independently | Preserve the observation; schedule its first later check under section 2 | Demonstrated once; later checking remains |
| Continue later checks | Preserve the same target/schedule identity | Ongoing maintenance; never permanent graduation |
| Pause a target/enrollment | Suspend offers in that scope; retain dates, needs and history | Paused, not complete or forgotten |
| Remove a goal or archive a curriculum | Append the enrollment revision; stop its offers | Previous goals/history remain inspectable |

Add `enrollment.practice: [{target: Ref, state: unstarted/active/paused, admitted_at: Time?, basis: Ref[]}]`. There is exactly one entry per `goal_order` target. `admitted_at` is the store-side decision time, not a learning timestamp. Unstarted entries have null admission time; active/paused entries require the actual admission decision. An unstarted goal stays unstarted even if its whole enrollment is paused. Pausing a previously admitted entry preserves its admission time; removing it preserves the old enrollment revision. Whole-enrollment status gates all entries without rewriting them. Re-adding a previously admitted exact target may reuse that recorded admission with a new enrollment decision; it never resets learning.

**Start this curriculum** opens a session preview using the selected budget and existing pending-work order. It does not admit every selected goal. The preview names the new targets covered by the proposed exercise/study and admits those targets atomically with starting that work. A sample exercise in the outline is only a preview, not a practice start. Self-reported familiarity selects a larger initial task instead of compulsory teaching; it supplies no success evidence.

Shared exact targets reuse one schedule and all compatible history. When beginning a second curriculum, its preview links already admitted targets into active practice in that enrollment; they need no repeated initial test. Merely importing/selecting that curriculum does not reactivate paused work. The target is offerable if at least one enrollment is active and has an active entry for it. A session offers it once within its selected enrollment scope. Pausing one enrollment cannot stop another active enrollment's work. No string-similarity merge or unreviewed equivalence is implied.

Changing scope after a combined task is open never edits its question, rubric or saved answer. Before delivery, reselection can replace an unshown draft. After delivery, offer **Finish this task** or **Leave it for later**. Finishing records actual evidence for all observed covered targets, including paused ones, but does not reactivate them or trigger follow-up practice there. Their preserved schedules may reflect that genuine evidence while offers remain suspended. Stopping preserves the draft; no response means no failure. A held/changed content contract can still prevent further delivery under the compatibility rules.

An incidental answer about an unadmitted target can remain historical evidence without creating ongoing obligations. Explicitly starting that target later considers compatible prior evidence under the same initial-success policy; it uses the real event time, not the admission time. If an old due time is already past, show it honestly. Unknown event time follows section 5.

### Counts and milestones

For the selected enrollment revision, `G` is the distinct exact selected target set, including held, paused and unstarted goals. Show selected count, unstarted count, active/paused count, demonstrated-at-least-once count and later-check needs separately. A current active-work view uses the explicitly labelled admitted/active subset; it must not call that subset the whole curriculum. Across curricula, deduplicate exact targets. Whole shared exercise effort remains counted once.

“Session finished” means the session ended. “All selected skills demonstrated at least once” requires a supported independent demonstration of every member of the frozen goal set under its contract; dates and later concerns remain visible. The current-work indicator is **“Caught up on later checks as of [time]”**, under `caught-up-1`. It is not a claim that every historical check succeeded or that maintenance is permanently complete.

Evaluate the indicator from effective history at the chosen source cutoff and evaluation time, without using later learning occurrences. For every target in the explicitly named scope, require all of the following:

- It is admitted and active in that scope, with a compatible accepted contract. An unstarted, paused or held selected goal prevents the whole-selected-curriculum indicator; an explicitly labelled active subset may still be caught up.
- It has an eligible independent successful later check, after its latest completed initial/repair teaching if any. Genuine compatible history can supply this evidence. A merely scheduled first check or immediate repair success cannot. Show “First later check pending” or “Later check after repair pending” as appropriate.
- It has no unresolved initial, repair, clarification, content, assessment or chronology hold, and no pending submitted owner. Retired obligations cannot hide an unresolved concern.
- Every **currently open** obligation is later checking with a known original due instant strictly after the evaluation time. An obligation is due at equality. Unknown due time prevents certification. An exposure-based offer deferral does not move the original due time or make overdue work complete.

Use section 4's effective obligation dispositions: `consumed`, `replaced` and `invalidated` obligations are terminal and excluded from the *current open-work* test. Their historical failures, replacement reasons and waiting-time basis remain visible. Every admitted target must still have exactly one current open obligation or an explicit held/initial need; losing the current obligation cannot yield a vacuous award. Thus a failed check replaced by teaching can be followed by a valid later success and a reachable caught-up state, without marking the old failure successful.

Save each awarded enrollment checkpoint with `id: UUID`, `target_refs: Ref[]`, `source: SourcePrefix`, `evaluated_at: Time`, `status: current/superseded`, `status_reason: text?`, and the existing label/rule/basis. Source is the complete prefix preceding the award command, using the data contract's existing source-identity type. The basis includes the enrollment scope revision, effective schedule/concern and qualifying answer refs; `achieved_at` is the award decision time, not fabricated learning time. A correction can append an enrollment revision marking the same checkpoint superseded with its reason; never delete the former award. Ordinary passage of time or new later work changes the live indicator but does not make a truthful earlier award false. A saved historical report remains immutable.

## 2. First evidence and the next check

Apply these rules per exact target, not once per larger answer. Scheduled answer transitions require an owned offer; extra answers retain evidence and may change concerns, but do not consume an existing opportunity. The explicit adoption of compatible prior evidence at initial admission is separate: it initializes a schedule from the actual historical event, never invents an owned review or repeats an existing interval. A required-criterion error supports repair; omissions and ambiguity support initial/clarification needs. Partial success does not demonstrate a whole target. Actual execution cannot be inferred from a written procedure.

| Effective event | Capability scheduling result |
|---|---|
| First fully supported independent demonstration, with no earlier study/due obligation | First later check at actual answer time +24 hours; no invented lesson or immediate retest |
| Initial supported error | Retain initial scope, open the supported concern and route to bounded repair |
| Initial omitted/unjudgeable evidence | Keep initial/clarification need; no failure or dated mastery claim |
| Completed initial/repair teaching, or relevant helped check | First later independent check at actual completion/answer time +24 hours; immediate verification remains a separate action |
| Eligible due independent success | Next capability check at actual answer time +7 days |
| Early, exposed or otherwise unqualified success | Keep the observation and unsatisfied obligation; it does not earn seven-day advancement |
| Correction or late assessment | Recompute using original occurrences, with section 4's ordering and ownership rules |

The existing bounded repair and no-evidence rules still apply. The 24-hour/seven-day values remain versioned starting policies. Relevant later exposure can postpone the next offer without changing the original due time. For a bounded anchor, use its trustworthy upper bound and disclose the conservative placement; unknown anchors require a prospective timing recovery, not the grading/import time. Direct recall uses its eligible actual review/rating stream, not these capability intervals.

## 3. Authoring is a checked operation before delivery

Retain two semantic operations, `draft_material` and `assess_answer`. Extend `operation` with `intent: initial_outline/exercise_variant/teaching_intervention/null`, `request_group: UUID`, `try_index: 1/2`, `acceptance: pending/accepted/rejected/stale/cancelled`, and `acceptance_reason: text?`. Only authoring has a non-null intent. Request-group/try index enforces at most one targeted regeneration for a failed authoring request; transport retries use the same command identity. A successful provider response is not itself acceptance.

Every authoring packet saves exact source/knowledge/target revisions, allowed scope and aids, intended learner action, applicable requirements, prior relevant examples/teaching/exposure, requested output and protocol version. Optional absent inputs are explicit; source text and embedded instructions remain data. Do not include hidden evaluation solutions in practice-generation packets.

| Intent | Additional inputs | Candidate output and acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| `initial_outline` | Source passages, learner purpose, candidate existing claims and explicit reuse/mapping decisions | Proposed knowledge/relations, targets, outline and representative exercises; source support, conditions, coverage and reuse checks before acceptance |
| `exercise_variant` | Accepted target/requirements, previous exercise/family, required variation, concern/verification scope if any, full-cost budget | Exercise plus its `design_review`; verify correct solution, valid alternatives, elicitation, freshness, comparability and cost disclosure |
| `teaching_intervention` | Supported concern or explicitly uncertain teaching rationale, failed requirement, prior approaches, actual response evidence | A teaching definition with explanation/worked example, conditions, source/derivation support and review; check relevance, correctness and scope before delivery |

Add a `teaching` definition record:

```text
{targets: Ref[], scope: [{target: Ref, requirement_ids: text[]}],
 concern: Ref?, content: text, approach: text,
 sources: Passage[], supporting_claims: Ref[], derivation: text?,
 authoring_operation: Ref?,
 review: {actor: Assessor, protocol: Algorithm, checks: [{name: text,
   result: pass/fail/unresolved, reason: text}], disposition: accepted/held},
 status: draft/accepted/held/retired}
```

Targets/scope are nonempty, requirements belong to those targets, and sources/supporting claims justify the explanation or explicit derivation. A failed or unresolved consequential check prevents acceptance. Null authoring operation means manually/curated authored content, with the same review requirement. Accepted generated content is labelled as provisionally reviewed under that protocol, not expert-certified truth. Preserve the rejected raw response in its operation; do not project it into learner state.

Extend `study` with `teaching: Ref` pointing to the exact accepted definition. Its existing `content` must equal that definition's delivered text. An accepted definition can exist before any study. Starting presentation creates a study with `status: started` and scoped exposure; actual completion appends `status: completed` with time evidence. Completion is an explicit learner completion action, not an inference from displaying text or elapsed time. Edits require a new checked teaching revision before delivery. No general mutable content blob is substituted after a response.

The next-action path is **accepted assessment → saved concern/selection → reuse suitable accepted teaching or author a candidate → acceptance → presentation → actual completion → bind a fresh verification to those completed study refs**. Assessment may suggest the need/rationale; it cannot create accepted teaching, study completion or a new exercise as an unchecked grading side effect. Answer-bearing assessment explanations obey the same exposure rules even when not presented as a lesson.

Recheck dependencies both when accepting output and immediately before delivery. A changed/held source, target or concern makes that request stale for current delivery. Preserve output and exact old refs, but do not stamp them with the new revision. A newly bound request/review is required. Cancellation appends its state; a late provider response stays attached to the cancelled operation and cannot deliver anything. Unrelated content and already saved responses remain usable.

On timeout or unavailable model, reuse only compatible accepted material. Otherwise show “This explanation/question is not ready” and offer another ready target or pause. A fresh intent does not evade the retry bound for the same unchanged request. A changed source, corrected failed check or learner-requested revision is a new request with its reason and predecessor linked in `inputs`. Save all model/authoring effort without claiming that authoring time improved learning.

## 4. One due opportunity, two tabs, and late grading

An idempotency receipt handles repeated delivery of one command. It does not decide what two genuinely different attempts mean. Add ownership for ordinary check opportunities using the same application transaction and store.

Extend `schedule` with `obligation: {id: UUID, kind: initial/later, origin: Ref[], status: open/consumed/replaced/invalidated, successor_id: UUID?}`, `consumed_by: Ref?`, and `hold_reason: text?`. The obligation identifies the requested check, independently of schedule revisions. Exposure deferral and missing/unusable answers preserve its open state. An eligible independent success consumes it once and creates the successor obligation in the next schedule revision. Completed teaching may replace an unsatisfied obligation with a new post-teaching obligation, explicitly as **replaced after teaching**, not satisfied; preserve its original waiting-time basis. Invalidated means its generating decision no longer has an effective basis. Schedule basis/reason and any correction transition explain each terminal disposition. `consumed_by` is non-null only for an effective consumed disposition; old consumed revisions remain historical when corrected. `successor_id` names the resulting/replacement opportunity when one exists, otherwise null with an explicit current need. For recall, **any eligible actual owned rating**, including Again, consumes its opportunity once and creates the next FSRS opportunity; success-only consumption is the capability rule. An atomic command appends terminal predecessor and current successor revisions. There is one effective disposition per obligation at a source cutoff, and at most one current open obligation per target.

Add an ordinary `practice_offer` record:

```text
{attempt: Ref, session: Ref,
 slots: [{target: Ref, obligation_id: UUID, schedule_at_offer: Ref,
          role: owner/extra}],
 state: reserved/submitted/settled/released,
 reserved_at: Time, released_at: Time?, reason: text?}
```

One slot exists per covered scheduled target. Atomically create the bound draft/offer and claim available obligations before delivery. At most one reserved/submitted owner exists for an exact target/obligation. Several target slots can share a larger attempt, with mixed owner/extra roles. All actual coverage still requires assessment; ownership is no success evidence.

Offer `state` summarizes the whole attempt: submission applies to all slots; it is settled only when every slot has a terminal disposition, and released only when all remaining reservations have been released. Effective pending/settled/released status is resolved per slot from its accepted assessment/review and scoped correction effects. One held or correction-released slot cannot stop already eligible other slots from settling once, erase their evidence, or release their ownership. This requires no duplicate attempt or changed frozen binding.

A competing start first offers to resume the existing draft. If the learner continues with a genuinely different task, save it as extra for occupied slots and label that choice; it cannot claim a second scheduled review of the same obligation. A stale client submitting a distinct answer is still saved and classified extra for any slot it never owned. `attempt.purpose` is only initial/clarification/verification/practice; it never carries due/extra. Practice derives effective scheduling role per target from the offer binding, release history and schedule correction effects, without rewriting the frozen answer. Do not discard the response or fabricate an owner grant after seeing it. Idempotent retries return the same attempt/offer, and atomic claims serialize competing starts.

Owner submission changes `reserved` to `submitted`; it keeps the slot while assessment/rating is pending. Timeout or elapsed wall time does not release a submitted slot. Stop/abandon can release an unsubmitted owner, preserving the draft and exposure; resuming it must recheck ownership and may become extra. If assessment is deliberately paused, use an explicit `release pending check` command: preserve its submitted answer, permanently disqualify that offer from schedule consumption, and release the slot. A late result remains extra evidence. Explain that this permits a new check without pretending the pending answer never occurred. Unknown timing/order may still hold advancement under the rules below.

Accepted judgment/rating settles the offer. An unusable, helped or too-early answer leaves the obligation unsatisfied; a later suitable draft can own another offer for it. A completed study can replace that opportunity; any outstanding owner of the replaced opportunity cannot advance the replacement. Changing ownership never transfers a frozen measurement endpoint to a new answer. Its abandoned assigned draft stays missing.

For direct recall, extra/practice offers do not show a schedule-rating control and cannot mint an FSRS review. A valid owned review records the actual learner rating after the answer, with eligibility evaluated at the answer interval. Its `occurred_at`/time evidence identifies that bound retrieval, while the later rating acceptance has its own record/receipt time; grading/rating latency must not move the retrieval anchor. A rating suggested in text on an extra attempt remains a note, not a manufactured review. Known help/error still informs the visible need; replay must not invent another FSRS rating to account for it. An initial recall stream needs its owned actual review; capability first-success rules must not manufacture its initial state.

### Effective event order and recomputation

Use one resolver for ordinary acceptance, late results, corrections and explicit scheduler replay. It operates only on retained effective assessments, actual reviews/studies/exposures and offer decisions; never calls the model or generates a learning occurrence.

1. Group by exact target/compatible stream and underlying episode, not criterion count. Keep the admission/offer bindings and policy in force for each decision. An older answer cannot be rebound to today's due opportunity.
2. Establish occurrence order from qualified nonoverlapping time intervals or an explicit causal relation, such as submitting A before starting its linked follow-up B. A client-supplied `follows` ref alone is not trusted timing. Rating arrival and grading completion are not learner occurrence order.
3. For exact equal timestamps, use original submission receipt sequence for deterministic processing, with a simultaneous-evidence marker. Equal-time conflicting outcomes cannot establish a later recovery or error streak; retain a concern until separate ordered evidence resolves it. Overlapping/unknown times that could change the result remain order-uncertain. Commit order may sort the evidence display, but cannot resolve that uncertainty.
4. Replay ordered events from the last unaffected basis. An owned eligible success may consume its bound obligation once; an extra success cannot consume it again. A later supported extra error still creates a current repair need, and a later extra recovery can change the concern without awarding a second interval. A known later unassessed response leaves an assessment-pending hold instead of letting an earlier result certify current recovery.
5. Where unresolved ordering could change interval/concern state, preserve candidate evidence and prior due date, set `need: held` with the reason, and withhold automatic advancement/“latest”/streak claims. Do not erase a supported possible-current error. Offer clarification of timing, a fresh prospective check, or ordinary practice. No exhaustive enumeration of arbitrary histories is required for this conservative first release.
6. Commit accepted assessment and resulting projection/concern/schedule revisions atomically. Save prior decisions and explain any changed due date as a late-result/correction recomputation, not newly learned material. Reuse an existing successor identity for the same target, actual generating occurrence, policy and opportunity kind; a changed due/basis is a schedule revision, not another retrieval or obligation. If a generating event ceases to qualify, mark its unsupported opportunity invalidated and preserve the old lineage. Apply the descendant rules below before choosing the single current obligation/need. Replaying the same effective inputs yields the same semantic state and does not append duplicate effects.

A success at 10:00 followed by an error at 10:05 therefore leaves repair pending whether grades arrive success-first or error-first. The early success can justify its historical schedule transition; it cannot clear the later error. This applies even when the second answer was an extra attempt.

A new prospectively bound check can resolve an order-uncertain old segment only when its start is known to follow all that segment's occurrences, its full required scope is checked and its current conditions qualify. Save that boundary and preserve the old ambiguity. Pending old assessments can revise historical interpretation; they cannot masquerade as events occurring after the new check. If an unbounded old occurrence might still follow the new answer, request a supported ordering clarification or retain the hold. An owned new recall review can restart from a disclosed conservative new engine state through an explicit policy transition; never synthesize or reorder unknown old ratings to rebuild FSRS certainty.

Restore preserves offers, obligation identities and consumption history. Submitted owners remain pending; unsubmitted reservations resume or release through the ordinary command. A stale browser cannot acquire ownership merely because timer activation changed. Exact receipt retries still work. Import validates at most one live owner, scope/ref consistency, release/settlement transitions and no duplicate effective consumption. It does not rerun an assessment or release reservations during read-only validation.

### Correction after later checks

Use `descendant-correction-1`. Historical offer bindings and observed answers never change. A correction can change their **effective scheduling consequence**, recorded separately in Practice-owned `schedule` revisions. The assessment `transition` only links the before/after records. This is a narrow exception for a genuine formerly owned descendant, not promotion of an extra attempt into a scheduled review.

Process retained occurrences in supported order from the last unaffected basis. At each descendant, first calculate the need and earliest eligible time from the corrected preceding history. Do not clear a later error, pending assessment or unknown ordering with an earlier success. Apply these rules per target slot; unaffected slots in the same larger answer continue normally:

| Descendant state at the correction transaction | Effective result |
|---|---|
| Its opportunity remains supported | Keep normal ownership and consumption; recheck actual conditions and revised due eligibility |
| Reserved but unsubmitted on an invalidated opportunity | End that slot's effective reservation. Keep its draft and historical binding. Offer “Continue as practice” or a newly bound current check; a later submission of the old draft is extra for this slot. Do not release other targets in the same attempt |
| Submitted, judgment/rating pending, on an invalidated opportunity | Preserve the answer and its historical owner role. Hold new scheduled offers for this target until assessment settles or the learner explicitly releases the pending check. Label it “Earlier history changed; this answer is still being assessed.” Release permanently prevents this offer from advancing a schedule |
| Completed or subsequently assessed formerly owned capability answer | It can re-establish the maintenance schedule only if it is fully supported independent success, still contract-compatible, at/after its originally offered due time and the earliest eligible time derived from corrected preceding history. Known ongoing aids, insufficient delay, incomplete scope, release or uncertain timing prevent this effect |
| Descendant with no scheduling effect | Keep all supported evidence and concern consequences. A known error still supports repair; a valid extra recovery may clear the matching concern. Neither alone creates an interval. Keep the corrected open need/due; unknown ordering/eligibility holds advancement with the existing prospective recovery path |

For a qualifying capability descendant, record **reanchored from a real later check**. Retire the preceding unresolved obligation as `replaced`, not satisfied; retain its waiting-time history. Reuse/create the successor generated by this actual descendant, due at its answer time +7 days under the existing capability policy. Do not pretend the answer consumed the replacement obligation or transfer its frozen endpoint. If later occurrences exist, continue replaying them before publishing current state. A bounded but non-exact answer must satisfy both timing bounds conservatively; an unknown bound cannot earn advancement. If corrected history instead establishes that this is the first fully supported independent demonstration, with no preceding study/due anchor, use the ordinary first-success rule: answer +24 hours, not +7 days. Distinguish that known absence of a prior anchor from an unknown possible anchor/order, which retains a hold and the prospective recovery path.

For recall, retain each otherwise eligible actual owned review/rating even when its historical `schedule_before` loses its basis. Replay the corrected actual rating stream with the pinned adapter from the unaffected state (or its genuine initial state), once per actual review. Original `schedule_before` and offer refs remain audit history; corrected engine inputs and resolution provenance belong to the Practice-owned schedule revision and its exact basis refs. Remove a disputed/ineligible review from the effective stream without deleting it or inventing a replacement rating. An unavailable engine or unresolved eligibility/order produces a hold, not a guessed due date. A hold is a valid committed domain outcome: save the accepted correction, affected projection, held schedule with prior due/state and evidence basis, and unaffected target results atomically. Technical failure or invalid writes roll back; domain uncertainty does not discard a valid assessment. No capability +7-day rule is applied to recall.

Add `schedule.resolution_rule: Algorithm?` and `schedule.check_effects: [{offer: Ref, disposition: preserved/reanchored/no_advance/pending/held, revokes_reservation: boolean, basis: Ref[], reason: text}]`. The containing schedule supplies the exact target; every referenced offer must have that target slot. Non-correction decisions use null/empty. Practice appends these decision deltas, including when the due date itself does not change; the application transition references their terminal/current schedule revisions. Entries name the exact pre-decision offer revision and govern effective reservations without mutating frozen target/obligation bindings. At source cutoff S, fold decisions in accepted schedule-revision order per exact target contract and stable offer identity (across its revisions), retaining the latest disposition and any prior permanent revocation. Later deltas cannot clear a revocation. Historical queries include no delta after S. Retiring an invalidated unsubmitted slot sets `revokes_reservation: true`; merely finding an accepted answer ineligible does not. An effective revocation or explicit pending release permanently prevents this offer/target from advancing, even after a later favorable correction. Do not infer that distinction by parsing prose reasons. Later settlement appends the Practice-owned schedule decision using the then-effective correction basis, with an application transition linking it. There is at most one effective scheduling effect per underlying target/review episode, including across repeated corrections. Correction, terminal dispositions, checkpoint changes and current state commit in one receipt; unrelated targets are not blocked.

**Worked result.** Study September 30 → owned success A October 1 → owned independent success B October 8 → correction of A to unusable on October 10. A's October 8 opportunity is invalidated. B met its original October 8 due time and the corrected September-study delay, so B re-establishes maintenance. Its successor remains due **October 15**, using B's actual October 8 answer, with no new review. The September-study obligation is replaced by the supported later evidence, not marked passed by A. If the same correction instead reveals relevant exposure ending October 8 at 09:00 and B answered at 12:00, B cannot earn the seven-day interval. Preserve the corrected obligation; B's own now-past retrieval is also exposure for the next check. With no later feedback/reveal, the next eligible time is October 9 at 12:00, so it is already eligible when the correction is accepted October 10. Any newer exposure can defer that offer again. Unknown exposure/order holds qualification. A later genuine error after B still leaves repair pending.

## 5. Exposure has an end, and uncertainty has a prospective recovery

Apply [assistance-evidence-1](../learning-observability/data-contract.md#known-assistance-with-uncertain-time) to the saved response before deciding independent success or advancement. A known relevant in-task hint remains help even with an unknown timestamp. Combine it with external aid availability/relevance; the local `Observation.assisted` flag alone cannot certify independence. Exact attempt/entry refs and exposure refs preserve the basis through corrections and restore.

Judge exposure relative to the answer's supported production interval, ending at submission, not grading/rating time. The answer being evaluated is not its own prior exposure: exclude that attempt's response/retrieval occurrence from the prior-exposure scan, while retaining actual answer views, hints or teaching during it. Earlier attempts remain relevant exposure for future checks. Otherwise every answer would incorrectly reset its own delay to zero. A feedback reveal after submission does not contaminate that answer. If response production time is uncertain and exposure might overlap it, qualification is uncertain; do not assume the entire answer was produced at submission.

Extend `exposure` with `availability: ended/ongoing/unknown`, `ended_by: Time?`, `end_basis: app_closure/learner_report/null`, and `end_evidence: Ref[]`. Existing `timing` continues to describe the actual observed/bounded/unknown presentation interval. An `ended_by` value is a justified upper bound, not a rewrite of a previously unknown opening/reading time. It requires an ended state, evidence and explanation; ongoing/unknown entries have null ended-by. An outside report or recovery boundary does not become a qualified app clock merely because the server saved it.

Answer-bearing views retain their presentation identity until closed. Record closure separately through an appended exposure revision. Opening yesterday and remaining accessible today is ongoing availability, not a 24-hour gap. Before an independent check, close controlled relevant panels across active views and record acknowledgements. If availability cannot be established after a crash or another tab is unreachable, mark it unknown and offer ordinary practice or a short confirmation that the relevant material is now closed. Availability is not proof of reading, but known relevant aids available during a no-aid task make it helped under that condition. Unknown availability prevents certification without converting a correct grade into an error.

Add `condition_checkpoint` for a supported prospective boundary:

```text
{targets: Ref[], prior_occurrences: Ref[], established_at: Time,
 purpose: exposure_ended/prior_occurrences_ended/both,
 basis: app_observation/learner_confirmation/mixed,
 statement: text, limitations: text[]}
```

Targets are nonempty; prior occurrences are the exact uncertain exposures/attempts/studies being bounded. The checkpoint records what is known **now**, for example “Those views are closed; the reported outside study happened before now.” It never supplies their missing historical timestamps. App evidence can establish only controlled app state; outside/old history requires the learner's bounded statement. Record the weaker basis plainly. Choosing **Start a later check from here** makes this a short product action, not a request to invent an old date. If the learner cannot establish the boundary, ordinary practice remains available and qualification remains unknown.

For prospective capability scheduling, replace an unknown latest-exposure bound by this justified ended-by boundary solely for *future* eligibility. Proposed next offer is `max(original_due_if_known, checkpoint +24h, any_newer_relevant_exposure_end +24h)`. If no due time could be established from old learning, create a prospective 24-hour check whose reason/basis is the checkpoint; leave the historical due time unknown. The same boundary can order an old uncertain segment before a new check as described in section 4. New exposure resets the prospective wait; unresolved ongoing availability still holds it. Non-answer-bearing navigation creates no new exposure.

An already fixed measurement plan keeps its original anchor/qualification rules. A prospective recovery cannot make its old endpoint eligible or turn an exposure-free-history claim true. A new plan/check can use the checkpoint explicitly. Saved reports remain immutable. A current recalculation may change the interpretation of earlier uncertainty only when new evidence actually supports that earlier fact, with provenance; the passage of 24 hours alone does not repair the past.

Always label the scope of knowledge: “after 24 hours with no recorded relevant exposure,” with the recorded outside-study/closure basis available. The app cannot establish that no unreported outside learning occurred. This is enough for a transparent operational baseline; it is not certification of an experimentally controlled interval.

### Shared content across different targets

Use `exposure-scope-1`. Assessment credit follows the target actually assessed; exposure follows the **content actually presented or retrieved**. They are separate mappings. Seeing “mavo means blue” is relevant to both recall directions even though a success in one direction supplies no achievement or review for the other. Explaining a procedure can expose a command separately tested by another target. Mere dependency, shared topic or graph adjacency is not enough to declare that another answer was revealed.

Retain the actual revealed text or exact presented excerpts and their checked knowledge references, using the data contract's exposure scope fields. Relevance decisions identify the affected target requirements and why the presentation supplies an answer, step or retrieval cue prohibited by their conditions. A checked background-only connection is `not_relevant`; an unresolved semantic connection is `uncertain`. A model may propose a scope under the existing checked authoring protocol, with its identity and limitations; a generated label alone does not establish relevance or irrelevance.

Resolve scope as follows:

1. Candidate targets include explicitly named targets, targets whose requirement/content refs intersect the revealed knowledge or content, and exact equivalents established by accepted mappings. Include explicit scoped outside reports. Do not traverse arbitrary prerequisite or similarity edges to propagate exposure.
2. For each candidate, use an accepted relevance decision for the exact target/content revisions and conditions. A missing decision is uncertain, not “no exposure.” A normal retrieval is relevant practice for its own bound target; cross-target relevance requires the revealed-content check. An unrelated target with no candidate connection is unaffected by this recorded presentation. Unscoped outside uncertainty remains disclosed; it cannot certify a fully controlled interval.
3. Before delivering an independent check, evaluate all retained candidate occurrences for that target, including earlier attempts/study and views from other curricula. Relevant open aids make the affected answer helped under its conditions. Relevant closed exposure can defer capability eligibility and disqualify a timing claim without changing a correct raw grade into an error. Uncertain relevance with potentially disqualifying timing withholds the affected qualification/automatic advance; it does not postpone unrelated targets or prevent labelled ordinary practice.
4. Accepting a new target or materially changed scope requires the same candidate check against retained history. New target T2 can therefore find older T1 feedback through their shared content. Save a new relevance revision against the original occurrence, not a new presentation or a newly dated study. If its old timing or relevance is unknown, preserve that uncertainty and use the existing prospective checkpoint when appropriate.
5. Corrections and report queries use scope decisions available at their source cutoff. They do not call a model to fill missing mappings or use today's mapping in an old saved report. A newly accepted mapping can change a new interpretation of an old event, with its original time and explicit reason. It cannot change a frozen target contract or create a learning event.

When an attempt/study already represents the underlying exposure, use its retained content/timing and create a linked exposure record only when the additional scope/availability data is needed. `related_occurrence` deduplicates the underlying event. A relevance revision does not restart the exposure clock; a genuinely new reveal does. Qualification resolves the affected requirement scopes independently, preserves valid unexposed evidence elsewhere in a larger task, and never infers a weakness from exposure alone.
