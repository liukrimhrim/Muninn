## Question

Choose candidate methods for descriptive progress, uncertainty, paired change, delayed outcomes, forecast calibration and comparison of learning policies. Address one learner, dependent episodes/target families, changing difficulty, adaptive selection, nonrandom missing checks, learning carryover and repeated peeking. State estimands, assumptions, defensible unit of analysis, data sufficiency, unavailable states and when to prefer a simpler summary. Propose numerical counterexamples that can falsify an attractive but misleading report; do not fit invented parameters as learner truth.
