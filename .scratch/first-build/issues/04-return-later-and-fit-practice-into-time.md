# 04 — Return later and fit useful practice into time (build slice 4)

Status: ready-for-human

Implements slice 4 of the [first-build plan](../../skill-maintenance/first-build-plan.md#build-order-and-exit-checks) on top of slices [1](01-one-saved-learning-encounter.md), [2](02-diagnose-and-repair.md) and [3](03-correct-knowledge-and-judgments.md), under the same setup choices (Python 3.12, FastAPI, stdlib `sqlite3`, plain HTML/JS, Anthropic `claude-opus-5-5` behind the provider seam). Governing rules: the plan's [recall-vs-capability table and capability transitions](../../skill-maintenance/first-build-plan.md#evidence-diagnosis-and-reviews); [everyday learning §3 exposure](../../skill-maintenance/everyday-learning-design.md#exposure-no-help-answers-and-delay) and [§4 session planning](../../skill-maintenance/everyday-learning-design.md#4-planning-a-session-within-real-time) (`bounded-session-1`); [lifecycle §1 counts](../../skill-maintenance/learning-lifecycle-contract.md#counts-and-milestones) (`caught-up-1`), [§4 ownership](../../skill-maintenance/learning-lifecycle-contract.md#4-one-due-opportunity-two-tabs-and-late-grading) and [§5 exposure recovery](../../skill-maintenance/learning-lifecycle-contract.md#5-exposure-has-an-end-and-uncertainty-has-a-prospective-recovery); [tracking contract](../../learning-observability/tracking-contract.md) (`routine-tracking-1`, `claim-outcomes-1`); [data contract](../../learning-observability/data-contract.md#historical-cost-boundaries) (`cost-cutoff-1`, `cost-membership-1`); [module design](../../skill-maintenance/module-design.md) M05/M07 and "the FSRS adapter is private to Practice"; [memory-model research](../../skill-maintenance/research/memory-model.md) (py-fsrs boundaries).

## Exit check (from the plan)

Use a controllable clock with the real store and scheduler adapter; restart across a due boundary. Assert every capability transition, including failed/hinted/unresolved checks. An immediate correction never advances a later-review interval. Combined evidence satisfies only its actual targets. Overrun and too-small budgets preserve unresolved needs. Then observe a genuinely elapsed later session separately (a human step; recorded below as open).

## Decisions

1. **One clock.** Every due, next-offer and window decision uses the store clock (`tx.now`); tests drive it with the controllable `Clock`, and `tests.dev_server` gets an adjustable offset (`POST /dev/clock`) so the browser check can cross a due boundary. Times are stored in UTC and shown in local time.
2. **Two scheduling paths, chosen at admission and kept by the schedule.** A `recall`-mode target gets a `recall` schedule (FSRS); every other mode gets the `capability` schedule. The schedule's own `type` decides how it replays, so a schedule never changes path silently. Format 1 is unreleased and no learner library exists yet (the live runs are still open), so `capability-interval` v2 (24 h, then 7 days) replaces v1 without a policy transition; once a real library exists a policy change needs a `policy` transition.
3. **Pinned FSRS behind a Practice-private adapter.** `fsrs==6.3.2` (PyPI, 2026-08-09, still the latest on 2026-10-25; API re-probed: `Scheduler.review_card(card, rating, review_datetime)`, `Card.to_dict/from_dict`). Policy `fsrs-recall` v1: default FSRS-6 weights, desired retention 0.9, **no learning or relearning steps** (every review is at least a day apart, so a same-session answer is never a second review), fuzzing off (replay is deterministic). Only `practice.py` imports `fsrs` (architecture test). Engine state is the card's JSON text in `schedule.engine_state` (records hold no floats); it is always re-derivable by replaying the effective reviews.
4. **Reviews are the learner's rating of an owned recall answer.** After feedback on an owned recall slot the page asks "How well did you remember?" — *forgot* → Again, *remembered with difficulty* → Hard, *remembered* → Good, *easy* → Easy (mapping `recall-rating-labels` v1, saved in the review). A judged error allows only *forgot* (a failed recall is never Hard). No rating control, and no review, for: extra or revoked slots, help used (or a hint whose display was not confirmed), unresolved/not-shown/misread judgments, a judgment under live dispute, an answer exposed too soon after seeing the answer (a later check, not the first), or a target the answer did not fully observe (a combined exercise rates only what it actually showed). Re-rating appends a review revision (`supersedes`, reason); the latest revision counts.
5. **Recall replay.** Practice replays the target's effective reviews in occurrence order through the adapter (Again consumes too): each consumes the current obligation and opens the next, due at the card's due time. A review whose judgment was later corrected to something its rating cannot stand on (for example "remembered" on a now-error answer, or any rating on a now-helped answer) stays saved but leaves the stream; nothing is invented in its place. An owned, rateable answer without a review holds the schedule (`need: held`, "waiting for your recall rating"). Completed teaching changes no FSRS state. Errors still open concerns and route to repair exactly as before.
6. **Capability transitions (v2), per exact target.** First independent demonstration with no prior due time → consume the initial obligation, check due answer + 24 h (a helped or unknown-help first answer sets a due time, so the next on-time independent answer earns + 7 days). Helped → obligation stays open, due answer + 24 h. Supported error → repair need (due unchanged); completed teaching replaces the obligation, due completion + 24 h. Missing/unjudgeable → need kept, no failure, no new interval. Owned independent answer on a later obligation: before its due time → nothing changes; at/after due and delay-eligible → consume once, next check answer + 7 days; at/after due but too soon after relevant exposure → nothing changes ("answered too soon after seeing the answer"). Extra answers never consume. Descendant re-anchoring (slice 3) now uses + 7 days when a prior anchor exists and + 24 h when corrected history makes it the first demonstration, and also requires delay eligibility.
7. **Exposure has an end.** Exposures are created `ongoing` and closed by an appended revision (`availability: ended`, `ended_by`, `end_basis: app_closure`). Hints close when their answer is submitted; the page closes feedback and material views when it is left (navigation or `pagehide`). The server never closes a view on a tab's behalf, since another tab may still show it. The outline's "What this rests on" and "Source text" panels record exposure for the goals they reveal. Unclosed exposures stay `ongoing`/`unknown` and certify nothing.
8. **One delay claim, owned by Evidence.** `delayed-independent-check` v1 `{minimum_delay_hours: 24}`: the latest relevant exposure before the answer (earlier answers' submissions, completed studies, and exposures naming the exact target; the answer's own retrieval excluded) must have ended at least 24 h before it. Unknown availability → uncertain; a `condition_checkpoint` bounds earlier unknown exposures at its time (prospectively only). Practice uses it for the capability advance, the recall rating gate and `schedule.next_offer`; Measurement uses the same claim (plus "in a later session") for tracking.
9. **Next offer is separate from due.** `schedule.next_offer {at, basis, reason}` = the later of due and the delay claim's eligible time, written whenever the replay or an exposure changes it. It also applies to a first (initial) check that already has an answer, so a missing or unjudgeable answer is not served again straight away (no loop); it is a deferral, not a new interval. A check that replaced an unmet one after teaching keeps the unmet check's waiting time. `due_at` never moves for an exposure; `at: null` means "an earlier view may still be open". Selecting such a check offers "It's closed — start a later check from here" (a learner-confirmed `condition_checkpoint`; the check becomes eligible 24 h later) or immediate practice.
10. **Ownership (M05).** A slot is `owner` only when no live (reserved/submitted, not revoked) owner holds that target's current obligation and the obligation is offerable now (initial, or now ≥ next offer); otherwise `extra`. A competing start resumes the existing draft. "Practice something else" (a learner override naming an exercise) delivers a different task whose occupied slots are extra and says so. A stale submission hits the revision conflict; the browser keeps its text.
11. **Selection: `bounded-session-1`.** Order: resume a reserved draft → a started study → concerns from this session's answers (current encounter) → older open concerns and ready checks by `waiting_since` (original due time; otherwise when the need arose), then session curriculum order, goal order, target id → new material (unstarted goals) when nothing pending fits or the learner chooses it. For checks, the plan must cover the first feasible need; plans compare by covered needs in priority order (lexicographic), then lower total full cost, then fewer exercises, then exercise id; only the plan's first exercise is delivered and the plan is recomputed after every action. No weighted sums. Unservable needs keep their waiting time and are reported with a reason (no exercise, held, too long for the time left, not eligible until …, waiting for a grade or rating).
12. **Costs and budget (`budget-elapsed-1`).** Measurement supplies the session's elapsed time; Practice applies the budget to it. An exercise's full cost is its setup + answer + feedback estimate (fit against it as an upper bound; status `estimated`). A clarification needs its own cost plus its worst branch's known cost (a fresh check sized like the original task); teaching has unknown length, so it is offered only while time remains and is labelled as such. Remaining time = budget − session elapsed time (breaks included; Measurement supplies it through Workflows, Practice never imports Measurement). Unknown time never becomes a saving. A spent budget stops offering by default (answers and feedback are never cut off); "add time" appends a session revision. A budget too small for anything says so. Ending a session saves the unmet needs with reasons; schedules are untouched.
13. **Sessions span ordered curricula.** `session.enrollments` is the chosen ordered scope; a shared exact target has one schedule and is offered once. The home page surfaces due work on open and can start a session over every curriculum with something due. A session starts from a short proposal ("10 minutes. Start with one overdue check. Two other checks may fit. New material remains available.") with a duration choice.
14. **Later checks view and `caught-up-1`.** Per curriculum: every later obligation with its original due time, next offer, how it ended (met on time, replaced after teaching, no longer applies) or that it is upcoming/overdue, and every answer that touched it (too soon, helped, unresolved, extra). The live indicator "Caught up on later checks as of [time]" is shown for the whole selected curriculum only when every selected goal is admitted, active, unheld, has a qualifying later success after its latest teaching, no unresolved need or pending owner, and every open obligation is a later check due strictly after now; otherwise an "active subset" indicator is labelled as such, or the reason is shown.
15. **Tracking (`routine-tracking-1`, `claim-outcomes-1`).** Preview (disposable) → "Start tracking" freezes, in one command: population (admitted active exact targets; held admitted targets included with reason; unstarted/paused listed outside), each target's baseline (last submitted attempt before freeze, with its judgment; missing stays missing), anchor (latest relevant occurrence before freeze, or null with reason), allowed exercise families, three 7-day windows from F, claim definitions, cost root (the enrollment ref) with `cost-membership-1` and investment window. At ordinary owned offer creation, Measurement assigns the window's still-unassigned endpoint to that draft/offer in the same transaction. Stop ends assignment. The report is live (`T` = now): raw `S/E/H/U/M/N` (a hint whose display was never confirmed keeps the raw grade and makes the claim uncertain) and, per claim (`performance`: Evidence's `independent-demonstration`; `later_check`: `delayed-independent-check` with `later_session`), the partition with every reason in the fixed order (contract, occurrence window, aid availability, minimum delay after the anchor, relevant exposure, later session); the formulas of tracking §5 including the QX ineligible/uncertain split, the missing-only range when it applies and the baseline→endpoint change pairs; period-to-date cost under `cost-cutoff-1` with unplaced and uncertain-membership time beside it; "Added since tracking began".
16. **Cost by enrollment identity (`cost-membership-1`).** A span belongs to an enrollment through its explicit `enrollment`/context refs, or through a single-enrollment session; a multi-enrollment session without attribution is reported as uncertain membership, never split. Displays clip reliably placed spans to their effective interval (`cost-cutoff-1`).
